The problem

A new coin trades against nothing. Its only reference is whatever the last buyer paid, so the price is pure momentum.

What it causes

Every pump needs a bigger pump. Fees go to the deployer in the coin itself, holders get nothing they can use, and the chart is the only story.

Liqio

A coin paired with a real stock or HYPE. The pair is where fees are paid and what holders are rewarded in, and the opening price is read from Hyperliquid's own market.

Pairs today

Three steps, one transaction

Pick a pair, name the coin, set the fee. The launch creates the coin and its pool in the same transaction, inside one HyperEVM block.

01

Pick a pair

HYPE for a 24/7 coin, USDC for a dollar pair, or a stock: NVIDIA, SpaceX, the S&P 500 and more, as xStocks by Backed on HyperEVM.

02

Launch at a known price

The coin opens at the market cap you choose. Its price in the pair comes from Hyperliquid's oracle at that moment, read on chain. No keeper, no signer.

03

Trade, earn, repeat

Buys and sells go through the pool. Your share of the fee accrues in the pair; holders can get a share too, paid out as HYPE on HYPE pairs.

Why Hyperliquid

Prices from the exchange itself

HyperEVM exposes HyperCore's oracle prices to contracts. Liqio opens every coin from that price, for HYPE and for the stock behind each xStock.

Stocks already on chain

Backed's xStocks are 1:1 tokenized shares with the same address on every EVM chain. They are here, with pools, so a coin can pair with NVIDIA the way it pairs with HYPE.

Blocks every second

A launch, a buy, a claim: each lands in about a second, for a fraction of a cent in gas. The coin is a minimal clone, so the whole launch fits in one small block.

Ready when you are

Launches open when the Liqio contracts go live on HyperEVM. Until then the markets show example coins so you can see how everything looks.
