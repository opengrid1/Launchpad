Overview

Introduction

Liqio launches fixed-supply coins on HyperEVM, Hyperliquid's EVM. Each coin is paired with HYPE, USDC or a tokenized stock and trades in its own Uniswap v4 pool with liquidity that cannot be withdrawn.

Liqio is live on HyperEVM. The addresses are on Contract addresses, with the source verified on HyperEVMScan and Sourcify. The pages here describe the next contract version, which removes every operator function; the contracts on chain today still carry them.
Liqio is a token launchpad on HyperEVM, Hyperliquid's EVM chain, and Equo's sibling on Hyperliquid. A launch is one transaction: it deploys a plain ERC-20 with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 coins, creates a Uniswap v4 pool for it against the paired asset the creator picks (HYPE, USDC or a tokenized stock such as wNVDAx or wSPYx), and puts the whole supply into that pool. One hook contract charges the swap fee on every trade and pays it to the creator, the platform and, if the creator chose it, the holders and a buyback.

1 billion fixed supply

Every coin is created with exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens, 18 decimals. The token contract has no owner, no mint, no pause and no tax.

Permanent liquidity

The whole supply seeds a single-sided Uniswap v4 position held by the factory. No function anywhere in the set removes it, for anyone, so it cannot be withdrawn. See the Security model.

Paired with HYPE, USDC or a stock

The pool quotes the coin in the paired asset: an NVIDIA-paired coin is priced in wNVDAx, a HYPE-paired coin in HYPE. The site trades every coin in HYPE through its router.

One hook, one fee

Every pool uses the same hook. It charges the fee the creator chose on every swap, wherever the swap comes from, and keeps the fee in the paired asset.

What the creator chooses

The launch form takes a name, symbol, description, links and logo, and the settings below. All of them are written on-chain at launch and cannot be changed afterwards, by the creator or by anyone else.

Where the fee goes

The hook takes the fee in the paired asset on every swap: wNVDAx for an NVIDIA-paired coin, HYPE for a HYPE-paired coin. If the coin has holder rewards or a buyback, those shares come off first. What remains splits 60% to the creator and 40% to the platform; a cashback for the trader and a referral share for the trader's referrer come out of the platform's part.
Part of the feeSharePaid in
Holder rewardsup to 50% of the fee, set by the creator (0 if off)the paired asset, pro-rata to balance, claimable any time
Buybackup to 50% of the fee, set by the creator (0 if off); holder rewards and buyback together at most 80%the paired asset, spent in the coin's own pool to buy and burn coins
Creator60% of what is leftthe paired asset, claimable any time
Platform40% of what is leftthe paired asset; the cashback and referral shares are paid from this part to the trader and their referrer
With holder rewards and buyback off, the whole fee splits 60% creator / 40% platform. The fee is the same whether a trade comes from the Liqio site, Uniswap or an aggregator, because the hook charges it inside the pool.

Tokenized stocks

Seven tokenized stocks and funds can be used as a pair today: NVIDIA, the S&P 500 ETF, SpaceX, GameStop, Micron, SK hynix and the Invesco QQQ, each as a wrapped xStock on HyperEVM. xStocks are tokenized stocks issued by Backed Finance and backed 1:1 by the underlying share; the wrapped form (wNVDAx for NVDAx, and so on) is an ERC-4626 share of the xStock, so one wrapped token is worth a little more than one xStock and the ratio drifts up over time. About 1,350 xStock products exist; Liqio lists the ones whose HyperEVM pools have depth. The stock tokens are issued by Backed, not by Liqio: Liqio shows them for information and does not issue, custody or redeem them, and the issuer's own terms apply to holding them. The stock's price follows NYSE hours while the pool trades at any hour, so a stock-paired coin's USD value can move while US markets are closed. The full list with addresses is on the Features page.

​
What Liqio is not

  • Liqio does not custody funds. Coins live in your wallet, liquidity lives in the Uniswap v4 PoolManager, and fees sit in the hook until claimed.
  • Liqio does not promise returns. A coin's price is set by trades in its pool and can fall to the opening price or below it.
  • Coins are created by their creators, not by Liqio, and are not securities issued by Liqio.
  • Nothing in these pages is financial advice. Read the Security model before you launch or trade.
