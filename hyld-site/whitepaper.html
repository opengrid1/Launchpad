HYLD whitepaper

HyperYield (HYLD) is a fair-launch token on HyperEVM. We don't tax trades, we didn't run a presale, and we hold no tokens ourselves. The whole supply goes into the trading pool as single-sided liquidity and the pool's 1% trading fee goes to holders. The liquidity is NOT locked: the deployer holds the LP and can withdraw it (the underlying HYPE and HYLD) at any time. Buyers must trust the deployer not to.

The fair launch

At launch, the full supply is seeded into a Hyperswap V3 pool as single-sided liquidity, in a price range that starts near a $5k valuation. We add no HYPE of our own. With only one token in the pool, there's no head start for anyone: everyone buys off the same pool at the same starting price.

When people buy, HYPE goes into the pool and HYLD comes out, so the HYPE side fills up entirely from buyers. The price starts low and climbs as more people join.

Rewards from the 1% fee

Every buy and sell pays a 1% pool fee, which is the highest tier Hyperswap V3 offers. The position keeps earning that fee in both HYPE and HYLD. Anyone can call collect, and 100% of whatever comes out goes to holders, pro-rata to their balance, paid in both HYPE and HYLD. Transfers aren't taxed and we take no cut.

Withdrawable liquidity (read this)

  • Liquidity is NOT locked. The supply is seeded as single-sided liquidity, but the deployer holds the LP and can withdraw 100% of it (the underlying HYPE put in by buyers, plus the HYLD) to any address, at any time. This is the single biggest risk: buyers must trust the deployer not to pull the pool. There is no timelock and no lock contract.
  • Token ownership renounced. The HYLD token contract can't mint new supply, pause trading, or freeze wallets, and ownership has been renounced (owner is the zero address). The deployer still holds the LP and keeps the liquidity-withdraw control described above.
  • Source verified. The token and manager contracts are source-verified on hyperevmscan. Read them before taking part.
  • Fixed supply. 10,000 HYLD, all of it seeded into the pool, with none set aside for us.

What to know

Since the pool starts with HYLD only, the early price is low and jumpy until enough buyers add HYPE to settle it. Read the contract and understand how this works before you take part. None of this is financial advice.
