Disclosures

  • Liquidity and controlThe Uniswap v4 position is held by the OneHook factory and is permanent: no contract has a function that removes it, so neither the creator nor the platform admin can withdraw liquidity. The creator cannot mint additional tokens, pause or freeze trading, rebase the supply, or otherwise control trading.
  • No reserved supplyNo tokens were sent directly to recipients or placed in a vesting vault at launch. The full fixed supply entered the pool; the creator bought 0.1 ETH in the launch transaction.
  • Anti-sniping protectionIf the creator switched on the anti-snipe instinct, buys in the opening window pay an extra tax that decays block by block from up to 30.00% back to the base fee. The launch block only accepts the creator's first buy. Sells are never taxed extra and can never be blocked.
  • Fee splitThe default hook fee is 1.00%, charged in ETH on every swap. After the coin's optional buyback and dividend shares, the rest is split 60.00% to the creator and 40.00% to the platform. The fee is capped by the constitution and can never be raised after launch.
