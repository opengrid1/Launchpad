How a war works

Every round runs the same 90 minutes, start to finish.

1Enlist

Coins launch in the first 15 minutes, each under a stock army: NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, SPY and more.

2Battle

45 minutes of open trading. Communities push their coin up the standings.

3Crown

The coin with the highest average market cap over the last 10 minutes wins. Late pumps barely move an average.

4Absorb

Losers' pools are pulled and buy the winner in tranches over 30 minutes. Losing holders redeem into the winner.
