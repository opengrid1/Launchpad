Select your army

Name your coin, pick an army, and enlist. Up to 8 fighters per round; enlisting is open for the first 15 minutes.

Launching costs gas only. The whole supply goes into a BNB pool; no presale, no team bags.

The rules

Win the roundAbsorb every loser's pool
Winner's holdersRewards in their army's stock
Losers' holdersRedeem for the winner
Who wins10-minute average cap
Trade fee2%, fixed forever

After the bell, every step runs on contract rules. Anyone can push the next step and earn a small tip.

How to play

Every round is the same 90 minutes.

Level 1 · 15 minEnlist

Coins launch into the round, each under a stock army: NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, SPY and more.

Level 2 · 45 minBattle

Open trading. Communities push their fighter up the board.

Level 3 · the bellK.O.

Highest average market cap over the last 10 minutes wins. A last-second pump barely moves an average.

Level 4 · 30 minAbsorb

Losers' pools buy the winner in 10 hits. Losing holders redeem into the winner.
