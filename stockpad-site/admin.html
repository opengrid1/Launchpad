Launch service

The service pays the gas of every leg a creator does not launch (about 7.7M gas each) and runs collects. Launch fees from creators land on this address on the chain they launch from. Keep each chain topped up.

Coins

Collect pulls the chosen share of a coin's pool liquidity (coins and native) on every chain it lives on, to the admin wallet. It cannot be undone. You sign a message; the service sends the transactions.
