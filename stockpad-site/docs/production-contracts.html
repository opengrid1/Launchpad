​
Factory

Launches coins (launch), holds every launch position (positionOf), records each coin's creator, pair, creation time and pool id (listings), exposes the opening market cap (startCapUsd8) and the pair price (pairUsdPrice), and carries the operator's housekeeping functions. It has no function that removes liquidity.

​
Hook

Attached to every pool with swap permissions only (beforeSwap, afterSwap and their return-delta flags). Computes and takes the fee on every swap, keeps the creator, platform and holder-rewards balances per coin, and pays them out when anyone calls payCreator, pushPlatform or pushDividends. feeBpsNow quotes the current fee.

​
Router

Trades any listed coin in ETH (buy, sell) or in its paired asset (buyWithPair, sellForPair), and converts ETH to a paired asset and back (ethToPair, pairToEth) along a caller-supplied route. The factory uses it for the dev buy. It holds no funds between calls.

​
Token deployer

Deploys each coin with CREATE2 at an address below its pair token's and transfers the supply to the factory. The coin reads its name, symbol, metadata and supply back from the deployer during construction, so every coin of a variant has identical creation code and verifies automatically.

​
Coins

Each coin is its own contract: a plain ERC-20 with permits (OneToken), or the variant with holder rewards (OneDivToken) when the creator enabled them. Addresses are returned by launch, listed by allTokens(i) and announced by the Launched event.

​
Price oracle

Returns the USD price (18 decimals per whole token) of WETH and each listed stock token from Chainlink feeds, and of other tokens from pool TWAPs. The factory calls launchPrice(pair) at launch and the site reads price(pair) for display. Its source is not part of the Equo set.
