Features

Pairs, the flat fee, anti-snipe, holder rewards and the dev buy: what each feature does and the bounds the contracts enforce.

A StockPad coin has five things a creator decides: the paired asset, the flat fee, anti-snipe, holder rewards and a dev buy. The first four are fixed at launch and enforced by the hook on every swap; the dev buy happens once, inside the launch transaction.

​
Pairs

A coin is paired with ETH (as WETH) or with one of the tokenized stocks below. The pair is currency1 of the pool, the asset the coin is priced in, and the asset every fee and reward is paid in. The router reaches each stock from ETH by swapping WETH to USDC on the Uniswap v3 0.05% pool and then USDC to the stock on the pool listed here.

The stock tokens are issued by Ondo, not by StockPad. According to the issuer they are not available to residents of the United States, the United Kingdom or Canada; StockPad shows them for information and does not issue, custody or redeem them. When US markets are closed the on-chain price of a stock token can drift from the price of the real share. The operator can block a token from being used as a pair for future launches; coins already paired with it keep trading.

​
Flat fee

A single percentage from 1% to 3% on the form (0.10% to 3% in the contracts), charged on every buy and every sell, measured and kept in the paired asset. The pool's own LP fee is 0, so the hook fee is the only fee the pool charges. The split is 60% creator / 40% platform after any holder-rewards share. The creator's share accrues in the hook and is paid out by payCreator(token), which anyone can call. Details are on Fees and anti-snipe.

​
Anti-snipe

An optional extra fee on buys during the first blocks after launch. The creator sets a start percentage (above the flat fee, at most 30%) and a window (5 to 7,200 blocks; the form takes minutes). In each block the buy fee is 
fee = start - (start - flat) * blocksSinceLaunch / window
so it falls in a straight line from the start to the flat fee and stays there once the window ends. Sells pay the flat fee throughout. Example for a 30% start, a 1% flat fee and a 150-block (30 minute) window:

The anti-snipe fee is split like any other fee. The creator's dev buy in the launch block pays the flat fee.

​
Holder rewards

An optional share of every fee, up to 50%, paid to holders in the paired asset. The hook puts the share into a pot per coin. pushDividends(token) on the hook, callable by anyone, moves the pot to the coin, which records it against the balances held at that moment. Each holder then claims with claimDividends() and receives the paired asset: ETH for an ETH pair, the stock token for a stock pair. withdrawableDividends(address) shows what a holder can claim.
  • Rewards are pro-rata to balance. Transfers carry no fee and are never blocked; the bookkeeping just keeps each holder's accrued amount unchanged as balances move.
  • The PoolManager (the pool's coins), the factory, the hook, the router and the coin contract itself hold no claimable shares.
  • A push needs at least 100,000 coins (0.01% of the supply) held by ordinary wallets; otherwise it reverts and the pot keeps accruing.
  • The share is fixed at launch and cannot be added, removed or changed later.

​
Dev buy

Any ETH sent with the launch becomes the creator's first buy. The router converts it to the paired asset (no conversion for ETH), the factory swaps it in the new pool at the opening price, and the coins go to the creator. The pool accepts no other buy in the launch block, so the dev buy cannot be front-run. The amount and the coins received are public in the DevBought event. The form allows up to 0.5 ETH.

​
What the hook also supports

The hook contract also implements a reflexive buyback, a per-wallet cap during an opening window, dynamic and MEV-tax fee modes and multi-stage parameter changes; StockPad launches do not use them and send the flat fee only. See Limits and validation.
