Known limits

  • The hook supports a reflexive buyback, a per-wallet cap during an opening window, dynamic and MEV-tax fee modes and staged parameter changes; Equo launches use the flat fee only and send those settings as zero.
  • The fee quoted by feeBpsNow and shown on the site assumes a priority fee of at most 1 gwei. A transaction with a larger tip pays the priority-fee surcharge on top.
  • A stock-paired coin's USD price depends on the stock token's on-chain price, which can drift from the real share while US markets are closed, and on the oracle's feed for that token.
  • Trading a stock-paired coin in ETH passes through the WETH/USDC pool and the stock's USDC pool; their depth bounds how much can be traded at once without large slippage.
  • Holder rewards are only recorded for the balances held when a push happens. Coins bought after a push earn from the next one.
  • The launch sends a minimum pair amount of 0 for the dev-buy conversion, so a stock-pair dev buy accepts whatever the route returns in that block.
  • The operator can hide a coin from the site's feed. The coin page and the pool still work.
