Overview

Introduction

Equo launches fixed-supply coins on Ethereum mainnet. Each coin is paired with a tokenized stock or ETH and trades in its own Uniswap v4 pool with permanent liquidity.

The contracts are live on Ethereum mainnet and every contract is source-verified on Etherscan and Sourcify; see Contract addresses. This documentation describes what the contracts do today.

Equo is a token launchpad on Ethereum mainnet. A launch is one transaction: it deploys a plain ERC-20 with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 coins, creates a Uniswap v4 pool for it against the paired asset the creator picks (ETH or a tokenized stock such as TSLAon or NVDAon), and puts the whole supply into that pool. One hook contract charges the swap fee on every trade and pays it to the creator, the platform and, if the creator chose it, the holders.

1 billion fixed supply

Every coin is created with exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens, 18 decimals. The token contract has no owner, no mint, no pause and no tax.

Permanent liquidity

The whole supply seeds a single-sided Uniswap v4 position held by the factory. No contract has a function that removes it, so it cannot be withdrawn.

Paired with a stock or ETH

The pool quotes the coin in the paired asset: a Tesla-paired coin is priced in TSLAon, an ETH-paired coin in ETH. The site trades every coin in ETH through its router.

One hook, one fee

Every pool uses the same hook. It charges the flat fee the creator chose on every swap, wherever the swap comes from, and keeps the fee in the paired asset.

What the creator chooses

The launch form takes a name, symbol, description, links and logo, and the settings below. All of them are written on-chain at launch and cannot be changed afterwards, by the creator or by anyone else.

SettingOptionsFixed at launch
Paired assetETH, or one of the listed tokenized stocksyes
Trading feeflat, 1% to 3% (the contracts allow 0.10% to 3%)yes
Anti-snipeoptional: an extra fee on buys in the first blocks, up to 30%, decaying to the normal fee over a window of up to 24 hoursyes
Holder rewardsoptional: up to 50% of every fee paid to holders in the paired assetyes
Dev buyoptional: ETH sent with the launch buys coins at the opening price in the same transactionone-off

Where the fee goes

The hook takes the fee in the paired asset on every swap: TSLAon for a Tesla-paired coin, ETH for an ETH-paired coin. If the coin has holder rewards, that share comes off first. What remains splits 60% to the creator and 40% to the platform.

Part of the feeSharePaid in
Holder rewardsup to 50% of the fee, set by the creator (0 if off)the paired asset, pro-rata to balance, claimable any time
Creator60% of what is leftthe paired asset, claimable any time
Platform40% of what is leftthe paired asset

With holder rewards off, the whole fee splits 60% creator / 40% platform. The fee is the same whether a trade comes from the Equo site, Uniswap or an aggregator, because the hook charges it inside the pool.

Tokenized stocks

Eleven tokenized stocks and funds can be used as a pair today: Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, Alphabet, Meta, GameStop, McDonald's, the S&P 500 ETF, the Nasdaq 100 ETF, the Silver Trust and the 0-3 Month Treasury fund, each as an Ondo "on" token on Ethereum. The stock tokens are issued by Ondo, not by Equo. According to the issuer they are not available to residents of the United States, the United Kingdom or Canada; Equo shows them for information and does not issue, custody or redeem them. When US markets are closed the on-chain price of a stock token can drift from the price of the real share. The full list with addresses is on the Features page.

What Equo is not

  • Equo does not custody funds. Coins live in your wallet, liquidity lives in the Uniswap v4 PoolManager, and fees sit in the hook until claimed.
  • Equo does not promise returns. A coin's price is set by trades in its pool and can fall to the opening price or below it.
  • Coins are created by their creators, not by Equo, and are not securities issued by Equo.
  • Nothing in these pages is financial advice. Read the Security model before you launch or trade.
