How it works

Three steps, one transaction.

01

Pick a stock

Choose the tokenized stock your coin trades against: TSLAon, NVDAon, SPYon and more, issued by Ondo and priced by Chainlink. The stock is the pool's quote asset.

02

Launch in one transaction

Name it, set a fee between 1 and 3 percent, optionally reward holders. The factory mints a fixed billion, seeds a Uniswap v4 pool and keeps the position with no withdraw path.

03

Earn in the stock

Every buy and sell pays the fee in the stock. Creators claim theirs any time; holder rewards, if enabled, accrue to every wallet that holds the coin.

Ready to list?

A launch costs gas and nothing else. Your coin opens on Uniswap v4 at a fixed market cap, paired with the stock you picked.
