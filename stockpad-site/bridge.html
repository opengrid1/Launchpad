How it works

1Burned on the source chain

Your coins leave the chain you send from. Nothing is wrapped or locked in a vault.

2Verified by independent checkers

LayerZero needs 2 of 4 verifiers (LayerZero Labs, Nethermind, Canary, Horizen) to confirm the transfer.

3Minted on the destination

The same amount, less the 0.2% fee, arrives in your wallet on the other chain.
