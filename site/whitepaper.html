HyperYield

HYLD whitepaper

HyperYield (HYLD) is a token on HyperEVM that pays its holders to hold. Every trade pays a small fee, and that fee goes straight back to holders as two rewards: more HYLD, and HYPE. There is no staking, no lockup, and no forms to fill in. Hold the token, earn on every trade, claim whenever you want.

How rewards work

Every buy and sell through the pool pays a 5% fee, taken in HYLD. Sending HYLD wallet to wallet is always free. That fee is split in two and handed back to holders the moment the trade happens:

  • Half as HYLD. Added straight to holders, in proportion to how much HYLD they hold.
  • Half as HYPE. Paid out in HYPE right away, even on buys, from a HYPE pool the token keeps topped up.

Rewards are tracked per token held, with no retroactive dilution: buying in later does not let you claim rewards from trades that happened before you held. What you earned stays yours even after you sell. You take both rewards together whenever you like by tapping claim.

The HYPE pool

HYPE cannot be created in the middle of a buy, so the token keeps a pool of HYPE to pay buy rewards instantly. The pool refills automatically by converting collected fees to HYPE whenever someone sells. If there is a long run of buys with no sells, the pool can run low for a while; the HYPE you are owed simply lands once the next sell tops it back up. The HYLD half of every reward never depends on the pool.

Fair by design

  • Fixed 10,000 supply. No minting, ever.
  • Tax capped at 5%. The owner can only lower it, never raise it past 5% on either side.
  • Pool and treasury excluded. They do not earn rewards, so they never eat into holders' share.
  • No lockups. Rewards accrue automatically; claim or sell anytime.
