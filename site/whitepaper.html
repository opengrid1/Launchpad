HYLD whitepaper

HyperYield (HYLD) is a fair-launch token on HyperEVM. There is no tax, no presale, and no team allocation. The entire supply is placed into the trading pool as single-sided liquidity, the liquidity is locked, ownership is renounced, and the pool's 1% trading fee flows to holders.

The fair launch

At launch, 100% of the supply is seeded into a Hyperswap V3 pool as single-sided liquidity, in a price range starting near a $5k valuation. The team adds no HYPE of its own. Because the liquidity is one token only, there is no insider entry: everyone buys from the same pool at the same starting price.

As people buy, HYPE flows into the pool and HYLD flows out, so the pool's HYPE comes entirely from buyers. The price starts low and rises as more join.

Rewards from the 1% fee

Every buy and sell pays a 1% pool fee, the highest fee tier Hyperswap V3 offers. The locked liquidity position keeps earning that fee in both HYPE and HYLD, even though the liquidity itself can never be withdrawn.

The LP position is held by an immutable locker that exposes only a collect function. Anyone can trigger it, and the collected 1% fees are forwarded to holders. No tax on transfers, no team cut, no discretion.

Locked and renounced

  • Liquidity locked. The whole supply is seeded as single-sided liquidity, then the LP is delivered to an immutable locker. The key is destroyed, so the liquidity can never be pulled.
  • Ownership renounced. Ownership is renounced in the same transaction the pool is created. No mint, no pause, no blacklist lives in the bytecode.
  • Fixed supply. 10,000 HYLD, all in the pool. None held back.

What to know

A single-sided launch starts with HYLD only, so the early price is low and volatile and then settles as buyers add HYPE. Understand the mechanics and the contract before taking part. Nothing here is financial advice.
