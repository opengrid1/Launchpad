HYLD whitepaper

HyperYield (HYLD) is a fair-launch token on HyperEVM. We don't tax trades, we didn't run a presale, and we hold no tokens ourselves. The whole supply goes into the trading pool as single-sided liquidity, that liquidity is locked, the owner is dropped, and the pool's 1% trading fee goes to holders.

The fair launch

At launch, the full supply is seeded into a Hyperswap V3 pool as single-sided liquidity, in a price range that starts near a $5k valuation. We add no HYPE of our own. With only one token in the pool, there's no head start for anyone: everyone buys off the same pool at the same starting price.

When people buy, HYPE goes into the pool and HYLD comes out, so the HYPE side fills up entirely from buyers. The price starts low and climbs as more people join.

Rewards from the 1% fee

Every buy and sell pays a 1% pool fee, which is the highest tier Hyperswap V3 offers. The locked position keeps earning that fee in both HYPE and HYLD, even though the liquidity behind it can't be pulled out.

A locker we can't modify holds the LP position, and the only thing it can do is collect. Anyone can call it, and whatever it collects goes to holders. Transfers aren't taxed, we take no cut, and there's no switch for us to flip.

Locked and renounced

  • Liquidity locked. The supply is seeded as single-sided liquidity, then the LP is handed to a locker we can't open. With the key gone, the liquidity stays in place for good.
  • Ownership renounced. The owner is dropped in the same transaction that opens the pool. After that nobody can mint new tokens, pause trading, or freeze a wallet.
  • Fixed supply. 10,000 HYLD, all of it in the pool, with none set aside for us.

What to know

Since the pool starts with HYLD only, the early price is low and jumpy until enough buyers add HYPE to settle it. Read the contract and understand how this works before you take part. None of this is financial advice.
