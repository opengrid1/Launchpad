How the launch works

Single-sided seed
All 10,000 HYLD goes into the pool at a starting valuation near $5k. The team adds zero HYPE. The price starts low and is set only by the pool.
People buy in
Each buy sends HYPE into the pool and takes HYLD out, so the pool fills with HYPE from buyers, not the team. The price rises as more join.
1% fee on every trade
Each buy and sell pays a 1% pool fee. Because the LP is locked, the position keeps earning that fee in HYPE and HYLD.
Forwarded to holders
Anyone can trigger the locker to collect the fees, which are forwarded to holders. No tax, no team cut, no key to pull the liquidity.

The HYLD Decrees

Answers for holders.
Why a single-sided liquidity pool?
It makes the launch genuinely fair. The team puts in zero HYPE, so there is no insider entry and no presale. Everyone buys from the same pool at the same starting price, and the pool's HYPE comes entirely from buyers.
If the LP is locked, how does it earn fees?
A Uniswap-style V3 position keeps earning its 1% trading fee even while the LP is locked. The locker exposes only a collect function that forwards those fees to holders. The liquidity itself can never be pulled.
Is there a tax, presale, or team allocation?
None. 0% tax on buys and sells, no presale, no team tokens. The only ongoing flow is the 1% pool fee, and that goes to holders.
What chain and exchange is this on?
HyperEVM, chain 999, on a Hyperswap V3 pool at the 1% fee tier, paired against WHYPE.
