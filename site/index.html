The HYPE pool can run low. Your HYPE is paid right away from a pool that refills when people sell. If lots of people buy and nobody sells, it can run low for a bit, and the HYPE you are owed shows up once the next sell tops it back up. Your HYLD reward never has to wait.

The tax can never go above 5%. The owner can only lower it, never raise it past 5%. The trading pool and treasury do not earn rewards, so they never eat into your share.
