How a reward reaches you

Four steps, all on-chain, all inside the token's transfer. Nothing is scheduled, manual, or off-chain. The function names below are the ones doing the work.

01
Trade taxed
A buy or sell through the pair takes 5% in HYLD. Wallet to wallet transfers are never taxed.
_taxFor()
02
Split in two
The tax divides by the reward split: part stays as a HYLD reward, part is owed as a HYPE reward.
_distributeTax()
03
Credited instantly
HYLD is credited at once. HYPE is paid from the buffer, so it lands even mid buy when the pair is locked.
_accrue()
04
You claim
Your share accrues per token held, with no retroactive dilution. Claim both rewards in one call.
claim()

The HYPE buffer can run dry. Instant HYPE on a buy is paid from a buffer that refills when someone sells. In a stretch of buys with no sells the buffer can empty, and the HYPE owed from those buys is paid once the next sell refills it. The HYLD reward never depends on the buffer.

Tax is capped at 5% and can only go down. The owner cannot raise it past 5% on either side, and the pool and treasury are excluded so they neither earn nor dilute holders.
