How the tax is booked

Trade is taxed
A buy or sell through the pair is charged 5% in HYLD. Wallet to wallet transfers are never taxed. _taxFor
credited as a HYLD reward
Part of the tax stays as HYLD and is credited to holders at once.
credited as a HYPE reward
The rest is paid as HYPE from the reserve, so it lands even mid buy when the pair is locked. _distributeTax

The HYPE reserve can run dry. Instant HYPE on a buy is paid from a reserve that refills when someone sells. In a run of buys with no sells the reserve can empty, and the HYPE owed from those buys is paid once the next sell refills it. The HYLD reward never depends on the reserve.

Tax is capped at 5% and can only go down. The owner cannot raise it past 5% on either side. The pool and treasury are excluded, so they neither earn nor dilute holders.
