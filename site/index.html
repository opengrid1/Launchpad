The HYLD Decrees

Answers for holders.
Why two rewards instead of one?
More HYLD grows your bag without you buying more, and HYPE pays you in the chain's own coin you can use anywhere. You get both from the same 5% fee, so every trade rewards you two ways at once.
How can HYPE be paid instantly, even on a buy?
The token keeps a small pool of HYPE and pays your HYPE reward from it right away. The pool refills automatically by converting collected fees to HYPE whenever someone sells. If buys outpace sells for a while the pool can run low, and the HYPE you are owed arrives once the next sell tops it up. Your HYLD reward never waits.
Is there a team tax or hidden cut?
No separate team tax. The whole 5% on each trade is split between holders as HYLD and HYPE. The pool and treasury are excluded from rewards, so they never eat into your share.
What chain and exchange is this on?
HyperEVM, chain 999. HYLD is paired against WHYPE on a Hyperswap V2 pool. Because the token has a trade fee it lives on a V2-style pool, not V3.
