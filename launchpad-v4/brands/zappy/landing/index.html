01 / The floor

A bid under every coin.

The floor is ETH the pool holds in a single tick under the price. It is sized to absorb every token outside the pool, so if every holder sold at once the price would stop on it.

  • ↑
    It climbs with trading. Once a block, the hook moves the floor as high as its ETH can carry.
  • ⇅
    Sells pay into it too. Every buy and every sell sends 1.5% of the trade to the floor.
  • 🔥
    Spare ETH buys back. When the floor holds more than it needs, it buys tokens and burns them.

04 / Anti-snipe

The first minute costs bots the most.

07 / FAQ

Questions.

What exactly is the floor?

ETH the pool holds in one tick just under the price, enough to buy back every token held outside the pool. If everyone sold, the price would stop there instead of going to zero.

How does the floor go up?

1.5% of every trade goes into it, and once per block the hook moves it as high as that ETH can carry. When it holds more than the holders need, the extra buys tokens back and burns them.

What does a trade cost?

2.5% of the ETH side: 1.5% to the floor, 0.6% to the creator, 0.4% to the protocol. In a coin's first 60 seconds the rate starts at 90% and falls to 2.5%.

How much supply is out early?

About 24% by a $100K market cap, 32% by $1M and 34% by $10M. The rest only comes out from $1B up.

Can the creator dump on buyers?

The creator holds nothing at launch except an optional buy of up to 5%, at the normal tax and shown publicly. That buy can also be locked and vested. Every other token is in the pool.

Which chain is it on?

Ethereum mainnet, on Uniswap v4. Coins pair with ETH or $ZAPPY.

Are the contracts public?

Yes. The source will be verified on Etherscan, and it builds on MIT-licensed open-source contracts.
