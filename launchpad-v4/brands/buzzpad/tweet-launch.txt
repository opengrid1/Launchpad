BuzzPad is now live on Ethereum mainnet.

BuzzPad is a meme coin launchpad built on Uniswap v4. When you create a coin, it goes straight into its own Uniswap v4 pool paired with ETH in the same transaction. There's no bonding curve, no waiting to graduate and no migration. Your coin is tradable on Uniswap from the very first block.

Every coin has a fixed supply of 1 billion and opens at a 1.75 ETH market cap. Liquidity is locked the moment it launches, so nobody can pull it.

How the fees work:

Every buy and sell pays a 1% trading fee. 70% of it goes to the creator of the coin and 30% goes to the protocol.

Creators can also add their own creator fee of up to 10% per trade, and all of it goes to them. Earnings can be claimed anytime from your profile.

Creators can turn on buyback. Half of their fee share is used to buy back their own coin from the pool. The bought coins are locked and released back to the creator slowly over 5 years.

Launching costs 0.0002 ETH plus gas. You can also make an opening buy of up to 5% of the supply in the same transaction.

Launch protection:

For the first 10 seconds after launch, buys pay a snipe tax that starts at 99% and drops fast to zero, so bots can't grab the supply before real buyers.

If the price moves more than 10% within a minute, an extra fee of up to 2% kicks in and fades out over 2 minutes. This slows down sudden pumps and dumps.
