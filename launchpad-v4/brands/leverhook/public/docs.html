01Overview

LeverHook lets anyone launch a coin on Robinhood Chain in one transaction. Each coin trades in its own Uniswap v4 pool, paired with ETH or with one of 50 Robinhood stock tokens such as NVDA, TSLA or AAPL.

Any coin can also be traded with leverage: open a 1.5x, 2x or 3x long with ETH as margin. The extra buying power is borrowed from the coin's own pool, so there is no vault to fill and no lender to wait for.

LeverHook never holds your keys. Every launch, trade and position is a transaction your wallet asks you to approve.

Before you trade
  • Names and symbols can be copied. Always check the coin address.
  • Prices come from each coin's live pool and move with every trade.
  • Leverage multiplies losses as well as gains. A 3x long is liquidated after a drop of about 25%.

02Launching a coin

Creating a coin deploys the token and its pool in a single transaction and places all of the supply into three liquidity positions held by the locker contract. The creator sets the name, symbol, image, description, links, the pair, and the wallet that receives creator fees.

  1. 01 / CREATE

    Pick a pair

    Choose ETH or a stock. The coin is minted with a fixed supply and its pool goes live in the same transaction.

  2. 02 / TRADE

    Spot or leverage

    Buy and sell from the first block. Leverage opens once the coin is 10 minutes old and its pool is deep enough.

  3. 03 / EARN

    Creator fees

    Every trade pays a 1% fee. 70% goes to the creator, paid out in the pair (ETH or the stock).

03ETH and stock pairs

An ETH-paired coin trades directly against ETH. A stock-paired coin trades against a Robinhood stock token, so its price follows both its own demand and the stock.

You never need to hold the stock. When you buy a stock-paired coin with ETH, the router turns your ETH into the stock (ETH → USDG → stock, through Uniswap pools on Robinhood Chain) and buys the coin in the same transaction. Selling goes the other way and pays you ETH.

A stock coin opens at the same value as an ETH coin, 1.75 ETH, counted in the stock at launch. Creator fees on a stock coin are paid in that stock.

Thin stock pools

Some stock tokens have little liquidity on Robinhood Chain. The ETH to stock leg of a trade can cost a few percent on top of the coin's own pool. The quote on the trade panel includes it.

05Launch protection

Snipe tax. For the first 10 seconds after launch, buys pay an extra tax that starts at 99% and halves fourteen times, reaching zero when the window closes. The proceeds follow the normal fee split, so a sniper pays the creator.

Volatility fee. When the price moves more than 10% within 60 seconds, the trading fee rises from 1% to 3% and decays back to 1% over 2 minutes.

Selling and wallet-to-wallet transfers are never restricted. Each coin keeps the terms it launched with; later setting changes apply only to new launches.

06Fees

Every buy and sell pays a 1% fee on the pair side of the trade, taken by the hook. The creator gets 70% and the protocol 30%. Fees are paid out directly in the pair currency, ETH for ETH coins and the stock for stock coins.

07How leverage works

A leveraged long lets you hold more of a coin than your margin alone would buy. With 1 ETH of margin at 3x, the pool lends you 2 ETH, and 3 ETH of the coin is bought and held for your position.

  1. 01 / OPEN

    Pay margin in ETH

    Pick 1.5x, 2x or 3x. The pool lends the rest and the whole amount buys the coin.

  2. 02 / HOLD

    Watch health

    The panel shows value, debt, P&L and your liquidation price at the live pool price.

  3. 03 / EXIT

    Close any time

    The coins are sold, the loan is repaid, and the rest comes back to you in ETH.

08Opening and closing

Open. On the coin page choose Leverage, pick 1.5x, 2x or 3x, enter your margin in ETH and confirm. For a stock coin your ETH margin is first swapped to the stock, and the pool lends the rest in the stock. The position records the coins bought and the amount borrowed.

Add margin. Adding ETH to a position pays down its debt and moves the liquidation price lower. Use it to keep a position safe during a dip.

Close. Closing sells the position's coins into the pool, repays the loan, and sends you the rest in ETH. If the coins are worth less than the debt, the position can't be closed by its owner; it is handled by liquidation.

All amounts on the page are shown in ETH, including for stock coins (converted at the live stock price).

09Liquidation

A position can be liquidated when its equity (value minus debt) falls below 10% of its value. Liquidation happens in two steps so a single wick can't wipe you out:

  1. 01 / FLAG

    Below the line

    Anyone can flag a position that is past the liquidation level.

  2. 02 / WAIT

    15 seconds

    If the price recovers or you add margin, the flag is cleared and nothing happens.

  3. 03 / LIQUIDATE

    Still below

    The coins are sold into the pool, the loan is repaid and the rest goes to you.

LeverHook runs a keeper that checks every open position each minute, so liquidations happen on time even if nobody else calls them. For a stock coin, the leftover and the keeper reward are paid in the stock.

Your margin is at risk

At liquidation most or all of your margin is lost. Watch the health bar and the liquidation price on the coin page.

11Where the loan comes from

A coin's liquidity sits in three price ranges. As the price rises past a range, that range is left holding only the pair (ETH or the stock). That pair is what leverage borrows: the locker lends up to 20% of it to open longs.

Every close and liquidation pays the loan back into the pool, and the borrow fee and liquidation penalty are added to it. Because the money is lent out of liquidity above the current price, the pool keeps the liquidity traders sell into.

This is why leverage needs a coin with some history: a brand new coin has nothing to lend until its price has moved up.

12Risk disclosures

Coins launched on LeverHook are user-created and experimental. Check the coin address, creator, liquidity and holders before you sign anything.

  • Prices can move fast and liquidity can be thin. Leverage makes losses bigger and faster.
  • A sudden drop can move the price past your liquidation level before you can react.
  • Stock-paired coins also move with the stock, and the ETH to stock conversion can be costly in thin pools.
  • Similar names and images can belong to unrelated coins.
  • Smart contracts, wallets, RPCs and indexers can fail.
  • Shown values are estimates, not execution guarantees.

LeverHook is an interface, not investment advice or a statement about any coin's quality. Stock tokens are issued by third parties; LeverHook does not issue them.
