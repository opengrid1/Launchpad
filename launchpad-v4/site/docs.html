How it works

One launch opens a coin on HyperEVM and on Solana, paired with HYPE on both. There is no bonding curve and no graduation: each chain gets a real pool from block one, and that pool is locked for good.

The launch

A launch is one transaction from your HyperEVM wallet. It deploys the coin (1,000,000,000 supply per chain, no mint, no owner, no transfer tax), opens a Uniswap v4 pool paired with native HYPE, puts the whole supply into three liquidity positions, hands the positions to the locker, and makes your opening buy if you want one. The Solana pool follows within about a minute, opened at the same price by the launch service and paired with HYPE on Solana. The two coins are bridged with LayerZero, so holders can move between chains.

  • Opening market cap: 50 HYPE on every chain.
  • Liquidity: 20% of the supply between 50 and 200 HYPE market cap, 30% between 200 and 1,000, 50% from 1,000 upward. No team allocation.
  • Opening buy: optional, exempt from the snipe tax, capped at 5% of the supply.
  • Launch fee: 0.005 HYPE. Addresses end in four zeros.
  • Name, ticker, image and description cannot change after launch.

Fees

Base fee1% of the HYPE side of every buy and sell, taken by the hook (Uniswap v4) or the program (Solana), never by the pool. 70% to the creator, 30% to the protocol. Fixed per coin at launch.
Creator feeOptional, 0 to 10% on top, chosen at launch and fixed. All of it goes to the creator and it is shown on the coin page.
BuybackOptional. Half of the creator's share buys the coin back from its own pool at each sweep; the coins vest to the creator over 5 years.
Volatility feeWhen the price moves more than 10% within 60 seconds, the fee rises to 3% and decays back to 1% over 2 minutes. A new move resets the decay. Same 70/30 split.
ClaimsFees accrue per coin, the operator sweeps them into the escrow, and the creator and the protocol claim from the escrow at any time. Nothing is held back and nothing can be redirected.

Snipe protection

During the first 10 seconds after a launch, buys pay an extra tax that starts at 99% of the HYPE leg and halves fourteen times across the window, reaching 0 at the end. The creator's opening buy is exempt. The proceeds follow the same 70/30 split, so a sniper funds the creator. During the window the volatility fee is off; it starts once the window closes.

Locked liquidity

On HyperEVM the three position NFTs are held by the locker contract, which has no withdraw function; the pool fee is 0 so there are no LP fees to collect. On Solana the Meteora DAMM v2 position is owned by the launch program, which has no withdraw instruction. The liquidity cannot be taken out by anyone, the protocol included.

Creators

The creator is fixed at launch. There is no takeover and no reassignment. The creator can change their own payout address, and that is the only thing a creator can change. Fees on Solana go to the Solana wallet given at launch.

Protocol settings

The protocol owner can set the policy for future launches (protocol share up to 50%, hook fee up to 10%, the creator fee cap, the buyback share, the snipe and volatility terms), the launch fee, the launch switch, a creator whitelist and the approved pair assets. None of it reaches a coin that is already live: every coin keeps the terms it launched with.
