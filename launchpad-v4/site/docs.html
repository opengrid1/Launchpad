Overview

LiquidFi is a place to launch and trade tokens on Elysium. You can browse launches, open any token to see its details, and trade straight from your wallet.

LiquidFi never holds your funds. Every launch and trade is a transaction your wallet asks you to approve.

Key facts
  • Names and symbols can be copied. Always check the token address.
  • Prices come from each token's live trading pool.
  • Launches can be volatile, illiquid, or lose all value.

How launches work

Creating a launch deploys the token and its trading pool in a single transaction, and the pool's liquidity is locked automatically. The creator sets the name, symbol, image, description, links, and fee wallet at creation.

Every token trades against HYPE in its own pool. There is no bonding curve and no migration later. Buys and sells happen in that same pool from the moment it launches.

  1. 01

    Create

    The token is minted with a fixed supply and its HYPE pool goes live in the same transaction.

  2. 02

    Trade

    Buys and sells run against HYPE in the locked pool and move the price.

  3. 03

    Earn

    Every trade pays a 1% pool fee. 70% of it accrues to the creator and 30% to the protocol.

Each launch uses a fixed supply of one billion tokens, a 1% pool fee, three locked liquidity positions (20% of supply from 50 to 200 HYPE market cap, 30% from 200 to 1,000, 50% above), and a small 0.005 HYPE launch fee. The opening market cap is 50 HYPE.

Opening buy

The creator can buy up to 5% of supply in the launch transaction itself, before anyone else can trade. That buy is exempt from launch protection.

Trading and pricing

Every token trades against HYPE in its own liquidity pool. The price you see is the live pool price, and it moves with each trade. The amount you actually receive can differ slightly from the quote. Slippage sets how much of that movement you accept.

Price
The current pool price for one token.
Market cap
Price multiplied by circulating supply.
FDV
Price multiplied by the full token supply.

The pool movement caused by the size of your trade.

The maximum execution movement your transaction accepts.

Assets available in the pool around the current price.

Launch protection

Buys are protected for the first 10 seconds after launch. During that window buys pay an extra tax that starts at 99% of the HYPE leg and halves fourteen times, reaching 0 when the window closes. The creator's opening buy is exempt. The proceeds follow the same 70/30 split, so a sniper funds the creator.

When the price moves more than 10% within 60 seconds, the pool fee rises to 3% and decays back to 1% over 2 minutes. A new move resets the decay. Selling and wallet-to-wallet transfers are never restricted.

Fixed at launch

Every token keeps the terms it launched with. Protocol settings only apply to launches that come after them.

Fees and buybacks

Trading generates a 1% fee on the HYPE side of every buy and sell, taken by the hook and never by the pool. The protocol keeps a share and the creator keeps the rest. The creator can claim their share from the LiquidFi interface at any time.

The split is snapshotted for each token when it launches and never changes afterward. A creator may add a creator fee of up to 10% on top at launch; all of it goes to the creator and it is shown on the token page.

With buyback & vest, half of the creator's share buys the token back from its own pool. Bought tokens lock and release to the creator's fee escrow gradually over 5 years; the protocol keeps its fee share of every release.

Locked liquidity

The pool positions are held by the locker contract, which has no withdraw function. The liquidity cannot be taken out by anyone, the protocol included.

Protocol revenue

The protocol's 30% share of fees funds infrastructure and the team. Nothing is held back from creators and nothing can be redirected after launch.

Risk disclosures

Tokens launched through LiquidFi are user-created and experimental. Review the token address, creator, liquidity, holder concentration, and transaction preview before signing.

  • Prices can move quickly and liquidity can be thin.
  • Similar names and images can represent unrelated tokens.
  • Smart contracts, wallets, RPCs, and indexers can fail.
  • Displayed values are estimates, not execution guarantees.

LiquidFi is an interface, not investment advice or a representation of token quality.

Onchain events

For a trust-minimized integration, index the factory's Launched event, register each emitted pool, and index its Swap events. Onchain events are the authoritative source of truth.

Event signatures are published here once the contracts are deployed.

Reading token state

Every launch is one token, one pool and three locked positions. Read the pool from the PoolManager and the positions from the locker; the hook exposes the fee terms and the protection window for each token.
