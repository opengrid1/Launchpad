Post 1
How rewards work on equo.fun

Every coin on Equo runs a Last-Buyer Pot on each of its EVM chains. Here is how you win it.

Post 2
The pot
0.1% of every trade on a chain goes into that chain's pot.

Buy $50 or more and you become the last buyer. That starts a 20 minute timer.

Every new $50+ buy takes the spot and restarts the timer.

Post 3
The win
When the timer runs out, the last buyer gets 80% of the pot, sent straight to their wallet.

The other 20% starts the next round, so the pot never starts from zero.

Sell before the timer ends and you give up your spot.

Post 4
The crash shield
If a coin drops 20% within 10 minutes, the shield turns on.

Sells pay up to 5% extra, and all of it goes into the pot. Buys pay up to half the normal fee.

It fades back to normal over 30 minutes.

Post 5
Creators earn too
Launch a coin and you get your share of the trading fee on every chain it lives on. On a 1% fee coin that is 0.7% of every trade.

Anyone can send your fees to you at any time. They always go to your wallet.
