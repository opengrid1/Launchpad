How rewards work on equo.fun

Every EVM pool has a Last-Buyer Pot. 0.1% of every trade fills it. Buy $50+ to take the spot and start a 20 min timer. When it ends, the last buyer wins 80% of the pot.

Creators earn a share of every trade on every chain.
