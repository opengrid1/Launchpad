Equo: one coin, every chain.

Launch a coin once and it goes live on 8 chains: Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, HyperEVM, Monad, MegaETH and Unichain. Same address everywhere.

How it works:

Launch. Pick a name, ticker and image, then set your trading fee from 1% to 5%. Every coin starts at a $3K market cap with its own pool on each chain. A dev buy is optional.

Bridge. It is one coin, not 8 copies. Move it between chains anytime through LayerZero.

Creator fees. You earn from every buy and sell on every chain, paid in the pair token.

Last Buyer Pot. 0.1% of all volume fills a pot. Any buy of $50 or more makes you the last buyer and resets a 20 minute timer. If nobody buys before it runs out, you win 80% of the pot and 20% rolls into the next round.

Crash Shield. If price drops 20% within 10 minutes, the shield turns on. Sell fees rise by up to 5%, and that extra goes straight into the pot. Buy fees get cut in half. The shield fades out over 30 minutes.

Dumps feed the pot. The pot pays the buyers. Trade from any chain, any terminal.
