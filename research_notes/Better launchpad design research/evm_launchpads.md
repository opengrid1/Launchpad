# EVM-Based Token Launchpads (Uniswap v4 hooks, Base, Ethereum mainnet) — mechanics and results as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-19. All numbers carry the date of the source where available. Primary sources (official docs, contract source, DefiLlama API) are preferred; where only secondary/promotional sources exist this is flagged.

---

## Key Question 1: What does Clanker's current v4 hook architecture look like (fee split, locker, LP ownership, vaulting, MEV module) and what changed across versions?

### Takeaway
Clanker v4 (Base, June/July 2025; v4.1 August 2025) is a non-upgradeable factory + custom v4 hook design: the full supply goes into one or more single-sided v4 positions held permanently by an LP-locker contract with no withdrawal path, the hook sets the LP fee per swap (static or volatility-based dynamic, capped at 30%), and the hook skims a fixed protocol fee equal to 20% of the LP fee, taken only in the paired token via beforeSwap/afterSwap deltas. Anti-snipe is a pluggable "MEV module" that runs for at most 2 minutes after pool creation (2-block delay, sniper auction, or parabolic descending fee starting up to 80%). Earlier versions (v0–v3.1) used Uniswap v3 pools with a 1% fee; v4's protocol fee was audited by Cantina and Macro (v4, Jun–Jul 2025) and Macro (v4.1, Aug 2025).

### Cited Findings

**Architecture / contracts**
- Core v4 contracts: "Clanker (factory)... handles token deployment, pool creation, LP placement through the LP locker, and extension triggering. It is 'non-upgradeable' and expected to stay unchanged for the life of v4." ClankerFeeLocker "accrues fees for all v4.0.0 tokens, and users can view and claim their fees." All implementations (hooks, lockers, extensions, MEV modules) "must be allowlisted on the factory by the team before use." — [Clanker docs, v4 core contracts](https://clanker.gitbook.io/clanker-documentation/references/core-contracts/v4)
- Hooks: ClankerHook (abstract base: protocol fee, automatic fee claiming, MEV module support), ClankerHookStaticFee ("sets a static fee for both the deployed token and the paired token"), ClankerHookDynamicFee ("adjusts the fee based on pool volatility"). v4.1 adds ClankerHookV2 with "Pool Extensions and MEV modules that can change the applied LP fee", plus StaticFeeV2/DynamicFeeV2. — [Clanker docs, v4](https://clanker.gitbook.io/clanker-documentation/references/core-contracts/v4)
- LP locker: "ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion: Supports up to 7 reward recipients and 7 initial LP positions. Each recipient chooses which token they want to accumulate fees in. It collects LP fees on every swap through the hooks and distributes them to recipients via the ClankerFeeLocker." — [Clanker docs, v4](https://clanker.gitbook.io/clanker-documentation/references/core-contracts/v4)
- Extensions: ClankerVault ("Vaults a portion of supply, with an optional linear vest after unlock. Minimum lockup is 7 days"), ClankerAirdrop (merkle root, lockup+linear vesting, min lockup 1 day), ClankerAirdropV2 (merkle root changeable after launch), ClankerUniv4EthDevBuy (deployer initial purchase with ETH). — [Clanker docs, v4](https://clanker.gitbook.io/clanker-documentation/references/core-contracts/v4)
- MEV modules: ClankerMevModule2BlockDelay (v4.0: "Keeps the pool non-swappable for the first 2 blocks after deployment"); ClankerSniperAuctionV0 (v4.0: "For priority-ordered L2s. Auctions the first swaps in a block, with auction fees going to token creators"); ClankerMevDescendingFees (v4.1: "Starting LP fee up to 80%, an ending fee, and a decay duration up to 2 minutes. The fee descends parabolically"); ClankerSniperAuctionV2 (v4.1: auctioned swaps pay the starting fee, descent begins after the auction ends). — [Clanker docs, v4](https://clanker.gitbook.io/clanker-documentation/references/core-contracts/v4)
- Deployment config: `DeploymentConfig` = `TokenConfig`, `PoolConfig`, `LockerConfig`, `MevModuleConfig`, `ExtensionConfig[]`; options include "Sending portions of the token supply to a vault or airdrop via Extensions; Splitting LP rewards between multiple recipients; Multiple initial liquidity positions with custom tick ranges; devBuys from the pool during launch; Choosing between static and dynamic fee pools." `Clanker::deployToken()` is "callable by anyone when the factory is not deprecated." — [clanker-devco/v4-contracts README](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts)
- Base mainnet v4.0 addresses: Clanker factory `0xE85A59c628F7d27878ACeB4bf3b35733630083a9`, ClankerFeeLocker `0xF3622742b1E446D92e45E22923Ef11C2fcD55D68`, ClankerHookDynamicFee `0x34a45c6B61876d739400Bd71228CbcbD4F53E8cC`, ClankerHookStaticFee `0xDd5EeaFf7BD481AD55Db083062b13a3cdf0A68CC`, ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion `0x63D2DfEA64b3433F4071A98665bcD7Ca14d93496`, ClankerSniperAuctionV0 `0xFdc013ce003980889cFfd66b0c8329545ae1d1E8`; v4.1: ClankerHookStaticFeeV2 `0xb429d62f8f3bFFb98CdB9569533eA23bF0Ba28CC`, ClankerHookDynamicFeeV2 `0xd60D6B218116cFd801E28F78d011a203D2b068Cc`, ClankerSniperAuctionV2 `0xebB25BB797D82CB78E1bc70406b13233c0854413`. Same factory address on Base, Base Sepolia, Unichain; Arbitrum uses `0xEb9D2A726Edffc887a574dC7f46b3a3638E8E44f` and a `ClankerMevTimeDelay` module instead of block delay. — [clanker-devco/v4-contracts README](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts)

**Fee mechanics (contract source, ClankerHook.sol)**
- Constants: `MAX_LP_FEE = 300_000` ("LP fee capped at 30%"), `PROTOCOL_FEE_NUMERATOR = 200_000` ("20% of the imposed LP fee"), `FEE_DENOMINATOR = 1_000_000`, `MAX_MEV_MODULE_DELAY = 2 minutes`. `protocolFee = uint24(uint256(lpFee) * PROTOCOL_FEE_NUMERATOR / uint128(FEE_DENOMINATOR));` — [ClankerHook.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/hooks/ClankerHook.sol)
- Protocol fee is "always taken in the paired (non-clanker) token", selected by `token0IsClanker ? currency1 : currency0`; the `initializePoolOpen` comment says the hook "will only collect fees on the paired token." Exact-input/paired-in and exact-output/paired-out cases take the fee in `_beforeSwap` (beforeSwapReturnDelta, fee minted to the hook as ERC-6909 claims); exact-input/paired-out and exact-output/paired-in cases take it in `_afterSwap` (`unspecifiedDelta = amountOut * protocolFee / FEE_DENOMINATOR`). — [ClankerHook.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/hooks/ClankerHook.sol)
- Auto-claiming: `_hookFeeClaim` runs first in `_beforeSwap`, burns the hook's paired-token claims and `take`s them to the factory ("Fees from earlier swaps are claimed on the next swap"); `_lpLockerFeeClaim` then calls `IClankerLpLocker(locker).collectRewardsWithoutUnlock(token)`. Pools use `LPFeeLibrary.DYNAMIC_FEE_FLAG`; native ETH pools are rejected (`ETHPoolNotAllowed`). — [ClankerHook.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/hooks/ClankerHook.sol)
- MEV window: `_runMevModule` only calls the module's `beforeSwap` if `block.timestamp < poolCreationTimestamp + MAX_MEV_MODULE_DELAY`; `_beforeAddLiquidity` reverts with `MevModuleEnabled` during that window. — [ClankerHook.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/hooks/ClankerHook.sol)
- Official fee table: "The Clanker fee is fixed at 20% of LP fees that are collected at the pool level in addition to creator LP fees." Creator 1% → protocol 0.2% → total 1.2%; 2% → 0.4% → 2.4%; 3% → 0.6% → 3.6%. "On deployment, the full token supply goes into a single-sided Uniswap v4 LP (the 'initial LP')." Rewards claimed on the token's Admin page on clanker.world. — [Clanker docs, Creator Rewards & Fees](https://clanker.gitbook.io/documentation/general/creator-rewards-and-fees)
- Uniswap hooklist registry entry (auto-generated, secondary): the hook "applies separate static LP fees for buying and selling", sets the fee via `updateDynamicLPFee` on each swap, takes a "Protocol fee: 20%, taken on the paired-token side of each swap via beforeSwap/afterSwap delta adjustments", and has a "time-limited" MEV module "active for up to 2 minutes after pool creation" that "can revert swaps". Callbacks enabled: beforeInitialize, beforeAddLiquidity, beforeSwap, afterSwap (+ both return-delta flags). — [Uniswap hooklist PR #3270 (ClankerHookStaticFee on Ethereum)](https://github.com/Uniswap/hooklist/pull/3270)

**Dynamic fee hook (ClankerHookDynamicFee.sol)**
- Constants: `MIN_BASE_FEE = 2500` (0.25%), `MAX_DECAY_FILTER_BPS = 30_000`, `FEE_CONTROL_DENOMINATOR = 10_000_000_000`. Per-pool config: `baseFee`, `maxLpFee`, `referenceTickFilterPeriod`, `feeControlNumerator`, `decayFilterBps`, `resetPeriod`, `resetTickFilter`. Fee formula: `variableFee = feeControlNumerator * (volAccumulator ** 2) / FEE_CONTROL_DENOMINATOR; fee = variableFee + baseFee`, capped at `maxLpFee`. The volatility accumulator is the absolute tick move from a reference tick (reset after `referenceTickFilterPeriod` of inactivity) plus a decayed carry-over of the previous accumulator; the post-swap tick is found by simulating the swap inside the hook (`simulateSwap` reverts with `TickReturned(tickAfter)`). — [ClankerHookDynamicFee.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/hooks/ClankerHookDynamicFee.sol)

**LP ownership (ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion.sol)**
- "Positions are minted to the locker" (`address(this), // recipient of position`); "No code path transfers the NFT out"; "No liquidity-removal function. The only DECREASE_LIQUIDITY call is in `_bringFeesIntoContract`, which uses zero liquidity"; "No time lock" (liquidity is permanently locked by absence of any unlock path, not by a timer). `MAX_LP_POSITIONS = 7`, `MAX_REWARD_PARTICIPANTS = 7`, reward BPS and position BPS must each sum to 10,000. — [ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/lp-lockers/ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion.sol)
- Fee conversion: each recipient has a `FeeIn` preference; the locker swaps the other token into the preferred one via Universal Router (`_uniSwapLocked`) or direct PoolManager (`_uniSwapUnlocked`), with `amountOutMinimum = 0` in the locked path. Admin functions `updateRewardRecipient`, `updateFeePreference`, `updateRewardAdmin` are gated only by `msg.sender == rewardAdmins[rewardIndex]`, "with no timelock". — [ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion.sol](https://github.com/clanker-devco/v4-contracts/blob/main/src/lp-lockers/ClankerLpLockerFeeConversion.sol)

### Inferences
- The v4 design moved the protocol take from an LP-share split to a hook-level skim on the paired-token side only; this means the protocol never accumulates the launched token and never needs to sell it (no protocol-induced sell pressure), while creators choose per-recipient whether to receive fees in ETH or the token.
- Because the locker has no decrease-liquidity path and the factory/hook are non-upgradeable, Clanker v4 liquidity is effectively "burned-equivalent" while still allowing fee collection — a pattern a mainnet launchpad can copy (hold the position in a locker with no unlock function rather than burning the NFT).
- The mutable `rewardAdmin`/`rewardRecipient` (no timelock) is the main admin-key surface; it cannot touch liquidity but can redirect future fees, which is what third-party scanners flag.
- Clanker's revenue is highly cyclical: Q1 2026 ≈ $35M gross fees vs. a mid-October 2026 run-rate of a few thousand dollars per day.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the v4 audit PDFs themselves (scope and findings).
- The exact descending-fee curve formula for ClankerMevDescendingFees and the sniper auction bidding mechanics were not retrieved from source.
- No official Clanker statement on Ethereum mainnet support was found; the evidence is the hooklist registry entry for ClankerHookStaticFee at `0x9bebe14d85375634c723eb5dc7b7e07c835de8cc` on chain 1 (listed Sep 5, 2026) and DefiLlama's $61.7K cumulative Ethereum fees.

## Key Question 2: How does Flaunch implement revenue share, automated buybacks ("Fair Launch", "Bid Wall"), and what results does it report?

### Takeaway
Flaunch (Base, Feb 2025) is a single v4 hook (`PositionManager`) that runs a fixed-price fair-launch window (30 min in app docs; 5 min default in the sniper-protection doc), then an ordinary v4 pool with a 1% swap fee that the hook captures in ETH (via flETH), splits by an immutable creator percentage (0–100%) and streams the remainder into a "Progressive Bid Wall" — a 0.1 ETH limit order placed immediately below spot each time 0.1 ETH of fees accrues. Reported 2026 figures are a single social post (~$472M volume, 151,995 tokens, 793 ETH creator revenue, 240 ETH buybacks) versus DefiLlama's $3.6M cumulative fees with near-zero activity in October 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Launch mechanism**
- "Each launch includes a 'Fixed-Price Fair Launch Window'"; revenue "Paid In ETH" to the creator; "Automatic Community Buybacks"; optional future launch time; optional "Treasury Manager". — [docs.flaunch.gg](https://docs.flaunch.gg)
- Fair launch: "Everyone gets the same price for the first 30 minutes." "A set number of coins are available to buy" at the fixed price; overbuying beyond the remaining fair-launch amount is "not protected by the fixed price"; coins bought during fair launch "can't be sold while it's active. Afterward, they can be sold at the purchase price minus fees"; creators "can buy some of their Fair Launch tokens before the public, at the same price." — [Flaunch docs, Fixed Price Fair Launch](https://docs.flaunch.gg/features/fixed-price-fair-launch.md)
- Flaunch parameters (SDK guide): initial market cap "must be greater than, or equal to, `1000e6`" (USDC, i.e. $1,000); fair launch allocation 1% (`1e27`) to 69% (`69e27`) of supply (examples 50% and 10%); creator fee allocation max `100_00` (two decimals; `6025` = 60.25%); flaunching fee "0.1% of the market cap set" (`PositionManager.getFlaunchingFee`); premine ≤ fair-launch amount, charged at swap price; `flaunchAt` ≤ 30 days ahead. — [Flaunch docs, Flaunching a Coin](https://docs.flaunch.gg/guides/flaunching-a-coin)
- Flaunch SDK/launcher statements: "flaunches below $10k market caps don't incur any protocol fees"; the Flaunchy launcher offers "$10K starting market cap, straight to market with 80% of trading fees, paid in ETH." — [flayerlabs/flaunch-sdk](https://github.com/flayerlabs/flaunch-sdk); [Flaunchy](https://docs.flaunch.gg/community/flaunchy)
- Sniper protection: creator-enabled CAPTCHA gate ("users must pass a CAPTCHA before they can swap") during the Fair Launch period, "5 minutes by default"; creators can set a per-wallet cap; Game Mode requires playing a game and purchase limit depends on score. — [Flaunch docs, Sniper Protection](https://docs.flaunch.gg/features/sniper-protection.md)

**Hook callbacks (PositionManager)**
- `beforeInitialize`: "We prevent external contracts from initializing a pool with this hook contract." `beforeAddLiquidity`/`beforeRemoveLiquidity`: block external liquidity changes during the fair-launch window. `beforeSwap`: "Only allows a swap if a premine call is available for a token scheduled to flaunch, closes the fair launch position once the window ends, and tries to fill swaps from fee tokens before they reach the Uniswap pool." `afterSwap`: "Once a swap has been made, we distribute fees to our LPs and emit our price update event." — [Flaunch docs, Hooks](https://docs.flaunch.gg/references/hooks)
- Newer vested-launch hook `AnyPositionManager` at `0xe753a351fb498051a09dc130fcc29aebc76525dc` (Base; listed Sep 17, 2026): callbacks beforeInitialize, afterAddLiquidity, afterRemoveLiquidity, beforeSwap, afterSwap, afterDonate, with beforeSwapReturnsDelta and afterSwapReturnsDelta; "`_beforeSwap` runs an internal swap pool that fills swaps against fee inventory before the AMM is reached"; fees captured via `_captureAndDepositFees` then `_distributeFees`/`_allocateFees`; optional referrer payout via hookData; pools revert `TokenNotFlaunched` until `flaunchesAt`; dynamicFee=false, upgradeable=false. — [Uniswap hooklist PR #8161](https://github.com/Uniswap/hooklist/pull/8161)
- Flaunch "uses Uniswap V4 hooks to wrap all the ETH into flETH via AAVE whenever a coin is bought" and says this lets it "charge zero fees at the platform level." — [Blocmates on Flaunch](https://www.blocmates.com/articles/flaunch-redefining-launchpads-with-fixed-price-fair-launch)

**Fee model / revenue share**
- "Once captured, the swap fee (1%) is distributed across 3 different recipients. One of these, the protocol fee, is optional." Waterfall: Protocol ("Optional fee set by proposal", 0–10%, two decimals so `550` = 5.5%) → Creator ("Defined when the token is flaunched") → Bidwall ("Defined when the token is flaunched"; if BidWall disabled, goes to `MemecoinTreasury`; "If the Memestream NFT has been burned, then this will be allocated to the protocol"). Worked example on a 100 ETH swap with 5% protocol fee: swap fee 1 ETH; protocol 0.0475 ETH; creator 0.722 ETH (80% of remainder); bidwall 0.1805 ETH. Each PositionManager has its own `FeeDistributor`; protocol fees sit in `FeeEscrow` claimable by `ProtocolFeeRecipient`; PositionManager1's protocol fees are "allocated immutably to the Flayer Foundation multisig" with "no obligation to use these protocol fees for buybacks." — [Flaunch docs, Protocol Fee Switch](https://docs.flaunch.gg/community/governance/protocol-fee-switch)
- Creator revenue: creator split "anywhere from 0% to 100% of the coin's revenue", "immutable and cannot be changed after the coin has launched"; "streamed on every swap and paid in ETH"; community share "streamed into auto buybacks"; creators can burn bought-back tokens or turn off buybacks to accumulate ETH; ownership of the revenue stream ("Memestream") "is tokenized as a transferable NFT." — [Flaunch docs, Creator Revenue](https://docs.flaunch.gg/features/creator-revenue.md)
- Fee switch governance: FLAY holders can enable a switch collecting "up to 10% of the Flaunch protocol's trading fees" via onchain governance (article ≈416 days old). — [Blocmates on Flaunch](https://www.blocmates.com/articles/flaunch-redefining-launchpads-with-fixed-price-fair-launch)

**Buybacks (Progressive Bid Wall)**
- "Whatever the dev doesn't take in fees goes to the PBW" (20% dev share → 80% to PBW). "A new PBW is created for every 0.1 ETH of trading fees it receives." Each PBW "places a 0.1 ETH limit order immediately below spot, reducing the impact of any selling." If price rises, "the bid wall follows it upward." Memestream owners can disable the PBW so ETH accumulates in the coin's treasury for approved actions. — [Flaunch docs, Auto Buybacks](https://docs.flaunch.gg/features/auto-buybacks.md)

**Audits**
- Omniscia (initial audit; full report not public, synopsis only), then three Enigma Dark reviews: follow-up to Omniscia findings "plus new zap logic, including flaunch scheduling and premining"; re-audit of flow optimisations; and "Version 1.1 contracts." The page says "three audits" but lists four. — [Flaunch docs, Audits](https://docs.flaunch.gg/protocol/audits)

**Results**
- Launch week (Feb 2025): "Flaunch has launched 2,135 tokens, generated $75.6 million in v4 trading volumes, and paid back 51 ETH since its inception last Friday." — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/uniswap-v4-goes-live)
- Social post (≈Aug 2026, unverified single source): "Flaunch alone reached ~$472M volume across 151,995 tokens, generating 793 ETH in creator revenue and 240 $ETH in buybacks." — [Mars_DeFi on X](https://x.com/Mars_DeFi/status/2092672499242917979)
- DefiLlama (fetched 2026-10-19): chain Base only; totalAllTime fees $3,606,857; total30d $17,589; total7d $11,078; total24h $411; daily fees Oct 13–18, 2026 between $0.29 and $12.52. Methodology: "Fees: Token trading and launch fees paid by users"; "SupplySideRevenue: The share of trading fees paid to token creators." — [DefiLlama API, flaunch](https://api.llama.fi/summary/fees/flaunch?dataType=dailyFees)
- DefiLlama Base launchpad category page lists Flaunch TVL ≈ $1.68M and 7d fees ≈ $11,076. — [DefiLlama Base launchpads](https://defillama.com/protocols/launchpad/base)

### Inferences
- Flaunch's PBW is the only design here that converts fees into protocol-owned *bid-side* liquidity (a limit order just below spot) rather than into full-range liquidity or buy-and-burn; this creates a visible floor but the 0.1 ETH granularity means low-volume coins never form a wall.
- The "internal swap pool" (filling swaps from the hook's fee-token inventory before touching the AMM) is how Flaunch turns token-denominated fees into ETH without a separate sell transaction — an architecture worth copying on mainnet because it saves a swap's worth of gas.
- Two different fair-launch durations appear in official docs (30 minutes vs. "5 minutes by default"), suggesting the default changed; the exact current value should be checked in `PositionManager.sol`.
- The $472M/152K-token figure is inconsistent with DefiLlama's $3.6M cumulative fees at a 1% fee (which would imply ≈ $360M volume) only if most volume occurred at reduced or zero protocol fee; it is plausible but unverified.

### Gaps
- Fair-launch supply default, the exact bid-wall tick placement, and whether BidWall tokens are burned or re-sold were not found in docs (Auto Buybacks page "doesn't say" who owns the bought tokens).
- No Flaunch deployment on Ethereum mainnet or any chain other than Base was found (docs index mentions Solana→Base bridging only).
- Audit PDFs and dates not retrieved.

## Key Question 3: How do Doppler's Dutch-auction dynamic-bonding-curve hooks work and who uses them?

### Takeaway
Doppler (Whetstone Research) offers three initializers behind an `Airlock` orchestrator: a *dynamic* v4 hook that runs a Dutch-auction bonding curve inside the hook (afterInitialize places slugs; beforeSwap rebalances once per epoch, moving the curve down by up to `(endingTick−startingTick)/numEpochs` if sales lag schedule; afterSwap tracks proceeds; beforeAddLiquidity reverts), a *static* single curve, and *multicurve* (a staircase of up to ~100 concentrated positions, optionally with a linear fee-decay anti-snipe hook, e.g. 80%→1.2% over 10 s, and a "rehype" hook that routes fees to buybacks/beneficiaries/LP). Liquidity can be locked in the initializer with a `NoOpMigrator` so it can never be withdrawn; Doppler takes a minimum 5% of fees. Zora, Bankr, Paragraph and Noice launch through it; Doppler claimed 6M+ pools (93% of v4 pools on Base) at its $9M Pantera-led seed in Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Dynamic (Dutch auction) hook — mechanics**
- "The entire auction runs inside the hook contract." `afterInitialize` "places the initial liquidity positions"; `beforeSwap` "rebalances the bonding curve if the current epoch hasn't been rebalanced yet"; `afterSwap` "updates `totalTokensSold` and `totalProceeds`" (swap fees excluded); `beforeAddLiquidity` "reverts external liquidity additions." — [Doppler.md (whetstoneresearch/doppler)](https://github.com/whetstoneresearch/doppler/blob/main/docs/Doppler.md)
- Rebalancing: against "a pre-defined sales schedule based on `numTokensToSell` over `endingTime - startingTime`"; if no swaps in an epoch, "the rebalance is applied retroactively to all missed epochs." Max Dutch auction (no net sales): curve shifts by `(endingTick - startingTick) / ((endingTime - startingTime) / epochLength)`. Relative Dutch auction (behind schedule): shift = shortfall percentage × maximum. Oversold: curve moves up by the delta between current tick and expected tick. `tickLower = startingTick + tickAccumulator`, `tickUpper = tickLower + gamma`. — [Doppler.md](https://github.com/whetstoneresearch/doppler/blob/main/docs/Doppler.md)
- Liquidity "slugs": Lower slug (from global `tickLower` to current tick, holds `totalProceeds` so buyers can sell back); Upper slug (from current tick by `epochLength / duration * gamma`, supplies the gap between expected and actual sales); Price-discovery slugs (contiguous, equidistant between the upper slug and `tickUpper`, creator-set count up to a max, each sized by time-fraction × `numTokensToSell`; none in the final epoch). — [Doppler.md](https://github.com/whetstoneresearch/doppler/blob/main/docs/Doppler.md)
- Official docs quote (via third-party model): "if the Protocol sold 80% of the expected amount… it will dutch auction by 20% of the maximum dutch auction amount." Recommended γ range `[1×, 3×]·(maxTick − minTick)`. Dynamic-auction state exposed by SDK: current epoch, total proceeds, tokens sold, minimum and maximum proceeds, `earlyExit`, `insufficientProceeds`, `endingTime`. — [holosko, Doppler auction math](https://paragraph.com/@holosko/i-rebuilt-dopplers-auction-math-from-the-whitepapers-what-the-curves-actually-do); [Context7 doppler-docs](https://context7.com/whetstoneresearch/doppler-docs)
- Doppler's docs call this "Doppler's Dutch auction bonding curve, aka Doppler v4"; Static and Multicurve are the other two "price discovery auctions". — [docs.doppler.lol llms.txt](https://docs.doppler.lol/llms.txt); [docs.doppler.lol, price discovery auctions](https://docs.doppler.lol/core-concepts/price-discovery-auctions)

**Multicurve / static / fee-decay / rehype**
- Multicurve "Replaces one constant-liquidity position with a staircase of `numPositions` concentrated positions"; configured with `marketCap.start/end`, `numPositions`, `shares`; author's model: single constant-liquidity curve "sells 17.7× more token0 in its cheapest bin than its dearest", multicurve with 100 positions ratio 0.10; modeled sniper-extractable value 80,000 vs 18,020 ("77.5% reduction"); practical range 50–100 positions. — [holosko](https://paragraph.com/@holosko/i-rebuilt-dopplers-auction-math-from-the-whitepapers-what-the-curves-actually-do)
- Official multicurve example: three market-cap curves, first "$500k to $1.5M... sets the launch price", `numerairePrice` 3000 with WETH; initial supply 1,000,000,000 with 900,000,000 sold; governance and migration `noOp`. — [docs.doppler.lol](https://docs.doppler.lol)
- Rehype hook (`withRehypeDopplerHook`): buckets "asset buyback 20%, numeraire buyback 20%, beneficiary 30%, and LP 30%", must sum to 1 WAD; "protocol beneficiary ... minimum of 5%" (example: protocol 5%, creator 95%); custom swap fee (example 3000 = 0.3%). Static auction example: ticks 175000–225000, fee 10000 (1%). Vesting via `withVesting({ duration, cliffDuration })`. Solana dynamic-fee example: `startFeeBps` 8000, `endFeeBps` 200, `durationSeconds` 600. — [Context7 doppler-docs](https://context7.com/whetstoneresearch/doppler-docs)
- Bankr's Doppler deployment (Feb 2026): Airlock `0x660eAaEdEBc968f8f3694354FA8EC0b4c5Ba8D12` (Base); `TokenFactory80` deploys DERC20; `ScheduledMulticurveInitializer` (Feb 10, start-time gate) replaced by `DecayMulticurveInitializer` (Feb 13); `UniswapV4MulticurveInitializer` "holds the locked liquidity position"; `NoOpGovernanceFactory`; `NoOpMigrator` "always reverts"; `StreamableFeesLockerV2` "locks positions and streams fees." Fee decay: start 80%, terminal 1.2%, 10 seconds, linear (`currentFee = startFee - (startFee - endFee) * elapsed / durationSeconds`), `FeeSchedule` packed in one slot. "Fees go to beneficiaries configured by the token deployer, plus 5% to Doppler." DERC20: up to 80% of supply vestable, max 2% yearly mint, ERC20Votes, permit. "Positions are locked in the initializer, not burned"; "Migration is effectively disabled"; "The hook blocks third-party liquidity additions"; current curve: one position holds 99% of supply over ≈ $27K–$1.66B market cap, 1% above range. — [Pastor Sanchez, Bankr→Doppler deep dive (Feb 24, 2026)](https://www.ignaciopastorsanchez.com/blog/bankr-s-migration-to-doppler-protocol-a-technical-deep-dive)

**Who uses it**

### Inferences
- In practice the integrators with volume (Zora, Bankr) use *multicurve + fee-decay* rather than the Dutch-auction dynamic hook; the dynamic auction is better suited to large, scheduled sales with a min/max-proceeds target than to instant memecoin launches.

## Key Question 4: How does Zora's creator coin model distribute fees to creators and why did it gain traction?

### Takeaway

Traction came from being wired into the Coinbase Base App (every post = a coin, creators get 50% of their creator coin vesting over 5 years), peaking at 118,069 coins/day in Jan 2026 before collapsing to 852/day and ~$112K/day volume by July 2026.

Backed by $ZORA"), Content Coins ("One per post, backed by the creator's coin"), Trend Coins (0.01% fee, unique tickers enforced on-chain). ## Key Question 6: Which designs have been audited or scored well on token-safety scanners, and which drew criticism (rug paths, admin keys, hidden fees)?

### Takeaway
Clanker (Quantstamp v2, Macro v3.1/v4/v4.1, Cantina v4), Flaunch (Omniscia + three Enigma Dark reviews), and Doppler (OpenZeppelin and Certora Nov 2024, Cantina contest, live Cantina bounty) all publish audits; Zora publishes none on the pages found. No launchpad-specific scanner scores were found. Criticism centres on Clanker's mutable reward admin (no timelock), launchpad fee stacks (Bankr/Doppler 1.75% all-in; Zora's former 3%), >95% of Clanker tokens losing liquidity within 48h (unsourced figure), Flaunch's PositionManager1 protocol fees going to a foundation multisig with no buyback obligation, and Four.meme's two 2025 exploits.

### Cited Findings
- Clanker audits: Jan 2025 Quantstamp (v2.0.0); Mar 2025 Macro (v3.1.0); Jun–Jul 2025 Cantina and Macro (v4); Aug 2025 Macro (v4.1). — [Clanker docs, Audits](https://www.clanker.world/docs/references/audits)
- Flaunch audits: Omniscia (initial, not public), Enigma Dark ×3 (incl. v1.1). — [Flaunch docs, Audits](https://docs.flaunch.gg/protocol/audits)

### Inferences
- All three hook-based protocols with audits share the same "safe-by-construction" properties scanners reward: fixed supply, no mint, no proxy, liquidity held by a contract with no withdraw function. Residual risk is fee-redirection (admin) and hook logic bugs, not classic rugs.
- A hidden-fee criticism applies more to *stacked* fee models (Bankr's 1.75% all-in on a "0.7%" headline; Clanker's "1%" that is really 1.2%) than to any single hook; a mainnet launchpad should present the all-in rate.

### Gaps
- No audit reports' findings were read (PDF links not fetched). No Zora or Virtuals audit page found in this research. No quantitative scanner scoring per launchpad exists in the sources found.

## Key Question 7: Is there any launchpad with sustained volume on Ethereum mainnet, and how do gas costs compare with Base?

### Takeaway
No Ethereum-mainnet launchpad with sustained volume was found. Ethervista (2024) now trades ~$54–77K/day almost entirely in its own VISTA token; Clanker's mainnet hook (live Oct 2025) has produced only ≈$61.7K of cumulative fees versus $92.2M on Base; new mainnet v4-hook launchpads (Epochpad, Unicurve, Hooky, BunkerPad, a hooklist "LauncherHook") show negligible activity (Unicurve ≈$55/day). Mainnet gas in Oct 2026 is very low (0.05–0.14 gwei; a swap ≈ $0.06), so gas is no longer the barrier it was; the barrier is distribution — Base launchpads are embedded in Farcaster/Base App/Bankr/Zora, and Uniswap Labs itself put its launchpad (pools.trade) on Robinhood Chain, not mainnet.

### Inferences
- The gas argument against mainnet launchpads has largely evaporated in 2026 (sub-0.2 gwei; swap ≈ $0.06), but mainnet still costs 100–1000× Base per transaction and spikes to $8–10 per swap at 20 gwei, which makes the Clanker-style per-swap auto-claim and fee-conversion swaps (extra PoolManager interactions on every trade) and the Flaunch-style per-0.1-ETH limit-order replacement materially more expensive than on Base.
- Every Base launchpad with volume is a *distribution* product (Farcaster bot, Base App, Bankr chat/X bot, Zora posts); a mainnet launchpad without a native distribution channel competes only on mechanism, which the Ethervista and Unicurve data suggest is insufficient.
- Mainnet's advantages a new launchpad could exploit: deepest ETH liquidity, Uniswap Labs' own hooks (StablePair/VolatilePair) proving mainnet hook pools can scale, and higher-conviction capital — i.e. it is better suited to fewer, larger, scheduled launches (Doppler dynamic auction / Flaunch fair launch style) than to pump-style spam.
