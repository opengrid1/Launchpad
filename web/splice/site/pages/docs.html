How OneHook works

OneHook is a launchpad on Ethereum. Every coin is born with composable on-chain behaviours called instincts, and they live in one immutable Uniswap v4 hook. The hook enforces a constitution no coin can break; each launch then chooses which instincts to carry.

One hook, not one contract per idea

Most launchpads ship a single fixed mechanic. OneHook ships one audited hook that supports every mechanic as a per-coin option. A creator picks instincts at launch; the hook reads them and clamps them to constitutional bounds in the swap path. Nothing about the safety core is upgradeable. The only choice is which instincts a coin turns on.

The constitution (always on)

These hold for every coin, regardless of which instincts it carries. No instinct can switch them off.

The instincts (pick per coin)

Optional behaviours a launch can build in. Each is read-only to the hook and bounded by the constitution.

The coin itself

The token is a plain fixed-supply ERC-20: one billion units, minted once, no owner, no mint, no pause, no transfer tax, no blacklist. All market mechanics live in the pool hook, never in the coin. The coin can only enter or leave the pool as part of a swap in its own pool or through OneHook’s own contracts, so nobody can stand up a shadow market for it, and the launch block only lets the creator’s first buy through so it can’t be sniped.

Fees

The fee instinct sets the swap fee, clamped to a hard ceiling. By default it is a flat ~1%, split 60% to the creator, 40% to the platform. A coin can instead carry dynamic or MEV-tax fees, and route part of its fees into a reflexive buyback or to holders as dividends.

Liquidity

Launch liquidity is paired with ETH and held by the one hook. Holders and creators can never pull it; the platform admin can collect from a pool's liquidity, and every collect is emitted on-chain naming the coin, the share and the recipient. The coin itself is a plain ERC-20: no owner, no mint, no tax, and nothing that can ever block a transfer. There is no bonding curve and no migration step: a coin trades in a real v4 pool from block zero.
