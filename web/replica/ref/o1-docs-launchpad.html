Overview

Introduction

Create a fixed-supply token with permanent Uniswap v4 liquidity and built-in fee sharing on Base, Robinhood, Monad, Arc, BSC or X Layer.

o1 Launchpad turns a token concept into a live onchain market. Configure your token, choose a supported paired asset, and launch with permanently locked Uniswap v4 liquidity. Base, Robinhood, BSC and X Layer have Standard and Tax suites for crypto or stock pairs. Monad offers Standard and Tax for MON, USDC and WETH pairs. Arc offers Standard and Tax for USDC and cirBTC crypto pairs. The create page shows the pairs available for your selected chain and token type.

1 billion fixed supply

Current launches create exactly 1 billion tokens with 18 decimals.

Opening FDV near USD 4,000

Current launches target an opening fully diluted valuation close to USD 4,000.

Permanent liquidity

The Uniswap v4 market opens at launch, and its liquidity is permanently locked by the launch contracts.

No paired-asset deposit

The complete fixed supply seeds the token side of the market without a creator ETH or quote-token deposit.

Stock-paired markets

Pair your launch with a supported Stock Token on Base, Robinhood, BSC or X Layer. Base, Robinhood, BSC and X Layer support both Standard and Tax products.

Current creation fees

Base and Robinhood launches currently pay a 0.001 ETH creation fee for every paired asset. Monad charges 100 MON, Arc charges 2 native USDC, BSC is configured for 0.003 BNB, and X Layer uses 0.02 OKB. Network gas is additional.

​
Standard and Tax tokens

Standard uses the platform’s configured creator, platform and referral fee split. Tax, deployed on Base, Robinhood, BSC, Monad, X Layer and Arc, lets the creator choose separate buy and sell taxes, then divide the remainder after protocol between creator funds, dividends and burns. The ordinary tax already includes protocol. Base Standard creates B20 tokens; Base Tax creates ERC-20 tokens. Both use fixed supply and permanent Uniswap v4 liquidity. Tax applies in the launch pool, not to ordinary wallet transfers or every unrelated pool. The six Tax deployments share the inclusive-tax model with chain-specific currencies and bindings. See Tax tokens for the launch and reward options.

From token setup to live market

1

Design the launch

Choose the token identity, paired asset, public profile, and profile-editing preference.
2

Create the fixed supply

The launch contracts create 1 billion tokens with no ongoing mint authority.
3

Open permanent liquidity

The complete supply is placed into the token’s Uniswap v4 pool, permanently locked, and available for trading immediately.
4

Use launch features

Use the features supported by the selected product: fee claims, profile tools, announcements, optional staking, and Tax dividends or burns where configured.
The Uniswap v4 pool is the token’s market from launch. Buyers exchange the paired asset for tokens from the locked position. As trading continues, the paired asset accumulates inside the same position and remains part of its permanent liquidity.
