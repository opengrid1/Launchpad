How payouts work

Every 3 days the top 5 traders on each board split a prize pool funded by 0.1% of all volume. Trade anywhere on Ink; the pool's hook sees it.

__COINS__Pool

0.1% of every trade on the platform, taken from the platform's share. Half to each board.

__TROPHY__Epoch

3 days. Payout at the end, then the boards reset. Top 5 per board: 1st 40%, 2nd 25%, 3rd 15%, 4th 12%, 5th 8%.

__SPARK__PnL board

Realized profit in USD over the epoch, from the ledger's cost basis. A wash trade is zero profit minus fees, so it cannot climb this board.

__ROCKET__Volume board

Fees paid in USD (volume × 2%). Washing $100K costs $2K in fees to compete for a share of a far smaller pool.

__SHIELDOK__Every trade counts

This site, Uniswap, aggregators, bots. The pool's hook records the wallet that signed each swap, so you do not have to trade here to rank.

__CHECK__Claiming

Payouts are claimable from Profile › Rewards in ETH and never expire. Minimum $100 of volume in the epoch to rank.
