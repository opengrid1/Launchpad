How payouts work

  • Pool0.1% of every trade on the platform, taken from the platform's share. Half to each board.
  • Epoch3 days. Payout at the end of the epoch, then the boards reset. Top 5 only, each board: 1st 40%, 2nd 25%, 3rd 15%, 4th 12%, 5th 8%.
  • PnL boardRealized profit in ETH over the epoch, from the ledger's cost basis. A wash trade is zero profit minus fees, so it cannot climb this board. Profit is only realized on tokens the same wallet bought.
  • Volume boardFees paid (volume × 2%). Washing 100 ETH costs 2 ETH in fees to compete for a share of a far smaller pool.
  • Every trade countsThis site, Uniswap, aggregators, Telegram bots. The pool's hook records the wallet that signed each swap, so you do not have to trade here to rank.
  • ClaimingPayouts are claimable from Profile › Rewards and never expire.
