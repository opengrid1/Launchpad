How it works

  1. Swap fee 0.3% on every trade. 0.05% of that is the protocol share, taken as LP tokens inside each pool.
  2. Collect Anyone can trigger it. The LP tokens are burned, both sides are sold for ETH on the AMM itself.
  3. Split The ETH is split across stakers by share at that moment. No admin in the loop, no owner key.
  4. Claim Rewards wait for you as ETH. Unstake any time; your rewards stay claimable.
